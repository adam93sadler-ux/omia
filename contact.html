Contact and support

Omia Player is made by an independent Android developer. Use the issue tracker for bugs, feedback and support questions.

Bug reports and feedback

Found a playback problem, a crash or a missing format? Open an issue on GitHub so we can track and fix it.

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The Omia Player version (shown in Settings → About)
  • What you tapped and what happened instead
Open an issue on GitHub

Diagnostic logs

Nothing leaves your phone automatically. If a bug is tricky to reproduce, you can export a playback log, playlist check or performance report yourself.

  • Open Settings → Diagnostics inside Omia Player
  • Review the file before sharing it, as it can include song, episode or playlist names
  • Attach the relevant lines to your GitHub issue
Read backup & log answers

Purchases and licences

Omia Plus purchases and yearly renewals are handled directly by Google Play.

  • To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings → Your licence → Restore purchases
  • Manage or cancel a yearly subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions
  • Request billing refunds directly through Google Play's order history
Read pricing questions

Privacy questions

Omia runs no servers and holds no user accounts or personal data. For details on permissions and external metadata lookups, read the privacy policy or raise a question on GitHub.
