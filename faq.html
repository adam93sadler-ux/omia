Files and setup

Does Omia include or sell music, videos or audiobooks?

No. Omia plays files already stored on your Android phone or local network, plus freely published podcast RSS feeds. It does not include, stream from subscription catalogues, or sell media.

Which Android versions are supported?

Omia runs on Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. Live glass blur on the floating bar and mini player uses Android 12 or newer; earlier versions use a translucent dark surface.

How does Omia access my folders?

Omia uses Android's standard folder picker (Storage Access Framework) and never asks for broad all-files storage permission. You pick the folders for music, videos and audiobooks, and you can revoke access at any time.

Can I move my podcasts from another app?

Yes. Import a standard OPML file or a Pocket Casts export under Podcasts → Add a podcast → More ways to add. You can also add individual RSS feeds directly.

Media and devices

Where do lyrics, covers and book details come from?

Synced song lyrics come from LRCLIB. Audiobook covers, authors and descriptions come from Open Library. Podcast discovery and song metadata lookups use Apple iTunes Search API and Podcast Index, and biographies come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. For library tools, Omia displays before-and-after changes for you to approve or reject.

Does Omia work with Android Auto and Google Cast?

Yes. Android Auto supports browsing your library and voice search in the car, and Google Cast plays audio and video to compatible TVs and speakers on your network. Both are included in the 14-day trial and Omia Plus.

Can I play files from a PC or NAS?

Yes. Network places connect to SMB shared folders and DLNA media servers on your Wi-Fi network. SMB credentials stay encrypted on your phone and are never copied into backups.

How do profiles work on a shared phone?

Profiles let each person have their own space on the same device, hiding or showing specific folders, artists, books or playlists. Your first profile is included free; extra profiles are part of Omia Plus.

Trial and Omia Plus

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Every feature opens as soon as you install Omia Player, with no account or payment details needed. The trial runs locally for 14 days so you can test the whole app with your own library.

What stays free after the 14-day trial ends?

Playing your own music and audiobook files, playlists you build by hand, backup and restore, and Lumen appearance settings stay free forever. Nothing you added or organised is deleted when a trial ends.

What does Omia Plus include, and how much does it cost?

Omia Plus costs £6.99 a year or £19.99 once for a lifetime licence (Google Play shows the price in your local currency). It includes podcasts, videos, synced lyrics, library tag and artwork tools, smart rule playlists, Google Cast, sound extras (equaliser, crossfade, even loudness), My Stats, extra profiles, SMB/DLNA network places, home-screen widgets and Android Auto.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google Play account and open Settings → Your licence → Restore purchases. Yearly plans also carry a 30-day offline grace period, and lifetime licences keep working offline once verified.

Privacy and backups

Does Omia collect analytics or require an account?

No. Omia has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your library, listening history and settings stay on your phone. Read the full privacy policy for every service the app connects to.

How do I back up my library to move phones?

Open Settings → Storage and backup to save a backup file to a folder you choose, then restore that file on your new phone. Passwords for SMB shares and private feeds stay encrypted on device and are excluded from backups.
