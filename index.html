One home screen for everything you were listening to

Music in one app, videos in another, podcasts in a third and audiobooks in a fourth splits your library apart. Omia keeps all four on one Home screen that opens straight to what you stopped last.

  • Pick up anywhere: your latest song, video, episode and book chapter sit ready at the top of Home.
  • Your folders stay yours: choose folders through Android's folder picker, with no broad storage permission.
  • Built for offline listening: play files on your phone, download podcast episodes, or stream from your own local network.

A player built for each kind of media

Each library keeps the controls that make sense for it. Switch tabs to inspect the Music, Video, Podcast and Audiobook players.

Music libraries built from your own folders

Browse by artists, albums, genres, songs, folders or playlists. Every screen takes its colour directly from the album artwork.

  • Manual and rule-based playlists: build lists by hand, or set rules by genre, artist, album, year, date added or time since last played.
  • Library mixes: Current favourites, Forgotten favourites, Deep cuts and Your mix assembled from your own listening history.
  • Synced lyrics and sound controls: line-by-line lyrics from LRCLIB with timing adjustment, equaliser, crossfade, even loudness, speed and sleep timer.

Local videos in the player, full screen or audio only

Watch your own video files with a single switch between audio-only listening, square player mode and full-screen viewing.

  • Continue watching: keeps your place across up to ten recent videos, with your last-stopped video always first.
  • Subtitles and audio tracks: switch embedded subtitle streams and alternate audio languages on the fly.
  • Video playlists and tags: group videos into playlists, filter with tags, and cast to your TV.

Follow shows and keep episodes offline

Search the podcast directory, browse charts, or bring every subscription over from another app with an OPML file or Pocket Casts export.

  • Up Next queue and downloads: new episodes arrive automatically and download over Wi-Fi for offline commutes.
  • Controls for spoken audio: 15-second back and 30-second forward skips, chapter support, playback speed and a sleep timer.
  • Private feeds supported: add RSS links directly, with feed passwords kept encrypted on your device.

Audiobooks with chapters, series and exact resume

Designed for long listens across multi-file folders and chaptered M4B books, keeping your exact position in every title.

  • Series shelves: group books into numbered series so the next volume is ready when you finish.
  • Chapters, bookmarks and skips: jump by chapter, drop bookmarks, change speed and set a sleep timer with shake-to-extend.
  • Built-in book lookup: fetch covers, authors, descriptions and publication years from Open Library.
