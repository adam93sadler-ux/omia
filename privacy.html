Omia Player: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Omia Player ("Omia", "the app") plays music, podcasts, audiobooks and videos that you choose. It is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

In short: Omia has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your library, listening history and settings stay on your phone. We don't run any servers, and nothing about you is sent to us.

1. Information that stays on your phone

To work, Omia keeps the following in its own storage on your device:

  • the list of files in the folders you choose to add;
  • playback positions, play counts and listening history;
  • playlists, profiles, podcast subscriptions and downloaded episodes;
  • lyrics, artwork and other details it has looked up;
  • your settings.

All of it stays on your phone. We can't see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless you have made a backup.

Backups. If you choose to make a backup, Omia writes a file to a folder on your phone that you pick. Where that file goes afterwards is up to you.

Crash reports. If Omia crashes, it may save a report to your phone so that you can choose to send it to us. Nothing is sent automatically.

2. Services the app connects to

Some features need to fetch information from the internet. When they do, Omia connects directly from your phone to the services below. Each one receives what any website receives: your IP address, a basic app identifier, and the request itself. None of them receives your library, your history or anything that identifies you to us.

Omia reads your music and books only from folders you choose through Android's own folder picker. You can remove its access at any time.

4. What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

5. Purchases

If Omia offers paid features, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We don't receive your card details. Google tells the app only whether a purchase is active. Google's privacy policy applies to payments.

6. Children

Omia is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. The app has no way to collect personal information in the first place.

7. Your choices and rights

All of the app's data is on your device and under your control:

  • clear it in Android's app settings;
  • delete backups you have made;
  • uninstall the app.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions about this, contact us (below).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/adam93sadler-ux/omia/issues.
